# Code of Conduct

ProtoSkipper adopts the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

## TL;DR

Industrial control is a small, professional community. We expect contributors
and maintainers to behave with the same respect they would extend to a
colleague on a substation site:

- Be welcoming. New contributors will not always know our conventions yet.
  Help them, don't ridicule them.
- Critique code, not people. Disagreement is healthy; personal attacks are
  not.
- Assume competence. The person on the other side of the issue tracker is
  almost certainly an experienced engineer working in good faith.
- Keep the tone professional. ProtoSkipper is used for commissioning real
  power and water infrastructure; the project's communication standard
  should match that seriousness.

## Reporting

To report a violation, email **conduct@datasailors.io**. Reports go to the
project maintainers and are handled confidentially. We will respond within
five working days.

## Enforcement

The maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and to
ban (temporarily or permanently) any contributor whose behavior is harmful.

For the full text covering scope, enforcement guidelines, and the four-tier
response ladder, see the canonical Contributor Covenant linked above.
